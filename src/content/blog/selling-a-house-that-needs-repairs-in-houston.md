## How to Present it to a Buyer

If you are trying to sell a house with repair issues, here are a few tips on how to handle it:

- Be honest with the potential buyer about what needs fixing and offer them a free estimate that you have researched on what it will cost. Then show them how you have subtracted this amount off of the asking price.
- Give the potential buyer a list of carpenters and other specialists and contractors that you have dealt with and show them how to contact them. This way, the buyer will think it will be an easy process to make the contacts to get started with the repairs after they purchase the house.
- Discuss whether the repairs are important for the welfare or value of the house. Buyers want to know that the house they are considering purchasing is going to be worth what they pay or more in the future. If they think the value will go down due to the repairs, they may want you to come down on the sale price.

## When Buyers Won’t Buy Due to Repairs Needed

There are times when you’ll find buyers who simply won’t purchase your house if it is in need of repairs. When you come up across these types of people, you will need to make a deal with them that usually involves throwing in extra things such as furniture or other perks, as well as lowering the amount of your selling price. Houses that have significant repair needs may be hard to sell because they represent added cost to the buyer.
