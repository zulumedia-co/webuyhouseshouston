![7 Great Things About Smaller Houses In Houston ](/blog-images/7-Great-Things-About-Smaller-Houses-In-market_city-c0f02731.webp)*Do you want to downsize your property in Houston? Learn more about the many great benefits smaller houses in Houston can offer you in our latest post!*

If you have lived in the same home for a long time, things have likely piled up. People accumulate things over time, rarely getting rid of as much as they are bringing in. This can make the thought of downsizing overwhelming. How do you decide what to get rid of and what to keep? How will you ever be able to live in a smaller space? The good news is, that once you get started, getting rid of things will be easier and you will be able to focus on all of the benefits of living in a smaller home. Whether you are an empty nester or just someone living in a large space, who’d like to rein things in a bit, downsizing your house in Houston is one of the smartest moves you can make. Below are just a few of the reasons why we love [smaller houses](https://www.moneycrashers.com/small-house-living/)!

#### Less To Clean

One of the things we hear most often from homeowners who have decided to downsize in Houston, is how grateful they are for the low maintenance lifestyle a smaller property can offer. With fewer rooms, you will have less to dust, less to vacuum, and less space to maintain. You’ll save time, money, and energy. Why spend your money cleaning up empty rooms? When you live in a house that is the right size, you will only have to clean up after yourself, no a bunch of empty space.

#### Few Places To Accumulate Junk

Going right along with having less to clean is having fewer places to hide away your junk. When you have stuff everywhere, you can feel cluttered both physically and mentally. When you live in a small space, there are fewer storage options, meaning you will have to reduce the number of items you own. Minimizing the things you own and keeping them orderly can have astonishing [mental health](https://rightasrain.uwmedicine.org/mind/decluttering-mental-health) benefits. Doesn’t feel great when you have cleaned out your closet or garage?

#### Lower Utility Bills

A smaller house in Houston means less to heat and less to cool. Your electricity bill will likely be much lower than that of a large house. And if you’re using cable, you might only have two boxes instead of three. A smaller yard means less to water and fewer plants to maintain. All in all, you can count on your utility bills going down when you own a smaller house. When you look at your cost savings for the year, you could be talking about thousands of dollars!

#### Cheaper Repairs and Upgrades

When you live in a smaller house in Houston, the costs to upgrade will drop dramatically from those of a large house. It costs much less to redo a small kitchen, recarpet fewer rooms, and to replace a smaller roof. Having a smaller house can save you thousands of dollars in upgrade and repair costs when all is said and done. With less space comes less for you to have to fix, maintain, and upgrade.

#### More Intimate

Many homeowners we meet talk about the large divide that living in a big house has placed on their family. When the kids are always off in the game room, basement, or den, it makes interacting with them much more difficult. You will find that you don’t see the members of your family very often, even if you are sharing one big roof. With a smaller house, families become closer both figuratively and literally. Living in close quarters will create a family bond that you can’t achieve with everyone so spread out.

#### Cost Savings

There are numerous ways a smaller house will save you money. Aside from the utility savings, you will also likely have a lower mortgage payment, lower property tax bill, and lower insurance costs. Your ownership costs for a small house will be much less than a large house of similar condition, in the same area. With all of the money you save, you can invest, travel, or leave something behind for your grandkids. Once you make the transition into smaller living, you will be surprised at what you are able to save and wonder why you didn’t do it sooner.

Downsizing your home may feel like a scary prospect, but it doesn’t have to be. You can feel good about getting rid of things by donating them or passing them on to friends or family members.
