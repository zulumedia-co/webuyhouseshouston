#### What Is Probate Exactly?

Probate is the distribution of a person’s belongings after their death. If you are dealing with a property tied up in probate, you know it can be a [long and frustrating process](https://www.legalzoom.com/articles/the-probate-process-four-simple-steps). If there isn’t a specified heir, the property will need to be sold in probate court. Either the deceased or the court will nominate an executor to handle the process. This is typically the person’s closest living relative. Once the house is sold, the proceeds will be split among the beneficiaries by the courts. The executor will also need to pay any debt, cash bequests, and fees. Not all properties will have to go through the probate process. With good estate planning, assets can be sold or passed down via a living trust.
