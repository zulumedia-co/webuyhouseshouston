## Rezoning Steps

**Review**

Review local ordinances before starting the process to [rezone a property](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/planned_urban_development.asp) in Houston. You must keep the point of view of city planners or any neighboring property owners in mind as you consider your rezoning plans; you don’t want to step too far out of sync.

**Apply and Pay**

Apply for the rezoning with the planning office, explain the plans you have and the details to rezone a property in Houston, and be prepared to pay the application fee with an acceptable payment method.

**Timing**

Pay attention to the timing of review board meetings and turn in your application before the cutoff date for the next meeting.

**Public Notice**

You may need to publicize the date, time, and location of the public meeting.

**On-Site Inspection**

Meet the rezoning assessor at the property

**Board Findings**

The zoning board should mail you a letter assessing their findings and any suggestions to be undertaken or disputed at the public meeting.

**Public Hearing**

Attend the hearing and await the final vote. As you plan your project to rezone a property in Houston and when you may get started, you should be aware that should the rezoning require official legislative updates, there may be delays after the approval.

**Appeal**

If the board denies your application, you can hire a zoning attorney and appeal the decision in court.

If necessary, you may incur costs for revisions to your rezoning plans and supporting documents, depending upon any suggestions from the board.

**Letters of Credit/Bonding**

Finally, appeals requiring legal assistance cost both time and money.
